Common Questions

Frequently
Asked

Travel

Do I need a visa?

Citizens of over 60 countries — including India under the e-visa scheme, the UK, USA, Australia, and EU nations — can visit Japan visa-free or obtain an e-visa on arrival for tourist stays of up to 90 days.

Getting Around

Is the JR Pass worth it?

The Japan Rail Pass grants unlimited travel on most JR trains including Shinkansen for 7, 14, or 21 days. If you plan to travel between Tokyo, Kyoto, and Hiroshima, it usually pays for itself quickly.

Planning

How much budget do I need?

Budget travellers can manage on ¥7,000–10,000/day. Mid-range travellers typically spend ¥15,000–25,000/day. Japan is often cheaper than expected thanks to excellent street food and affordable transport.
